# Outbreak: Lore

The story of the outbreak, told in found notes. This file is the canon: the notes in
`server/src/game/notes_data.zig` follow it, and anything new has to agree with it. Fiction only: no
real companies, people or tragedies. Tone is grim and human, with real dark humour (PG-13: light to
moderate swearing, gross-out, absurdity; no slurs, nothing explicit).

## The short version

**Halvorsen Biologics**, a mid-size biotech in a business park outside the city, licensed a fungus
recovered from thawing Siberian permafrost. The catalogue number is **KB-7**; everyone calls it "the
Kessler bloom" after **Dr. Ilse Kessler**, the mycologist who first cultured it. Halvorsen's program,
**Project Lantern**, used it to keep donor organs alive without oxygen, and it worked spectacularly:
tissue stayed warm and active for weeks.

KB-7 runs hot. Infected tissue sits at about 41 degrees C, which is why the Turned perceive the world
as heat (the Zombie Heat lens) and why the living glow to them. It spreads by bite. It quiets the
cortex and amplifies hunger. The infected are not "dead": they are fevered and driven, and
fragments of who they were still surface.

## The ambiguity (keep it)

Dale Pruitt is (probably) Patient Zero. The Patient Zero set says otherwise, or at least not only:
the first exposure was Kessler's lab tech, **Tomas Reyes**, three weeks before the crash, and
Halvorsen buried it. Whether Dale was infected by the cooler, by Reyes, or was simply made the
story ("the courier has a cut hand and an unlucky name") is never settled. The Sublevel 2 door was
found open the morning of the crash, opened from the inside. Nobody says where Reyes went. Do not
resolve any of this.

## The Survey

The county's volunteer emergency-mapping corps (retirees, surveyors, scout leaders, one dentist)
marked printed municipal maps with grease pencil for whoever came after. That is the Human Survey
lens. Their leader signs as **"Marguerite, Survey Lead, Grid 4"**. Their marks: circle (safe for a
night), cross (gone), plus (water), wave (a gathering), caret (high ground), two marks (not at night).

## Sanctuaries

Hospitals, schools and churches are where the infected will not go. Nobody knows why. The notes
disagree: the incense, the disinfectant, the floor wax, the quiet. A few hint that KB-7 "remembers"
the clean room it was cultured in. Keep it a mystery.

## Characters

- **Dr. Ilse Kessler**: mycologist. Cultured KB-7; named nothing on principle and named it anyway.
  Warned everyone. Last seen in the loading dock watching the van go.
- **Tomas Reyes**: Kessler's lab tech. The real first exposure. Kind, scared, says "I'm just warm".
- **Gwen Aldridge**: Halvorsen VP Strategy. Wrote the protocol she then claimed to have followed.
- **Dr. P. Okafor**: chair of the research ethics board. Said no.
- **Dale Pruitt**: courier. Cut his hand, ordered pie, texted his sister.
- **Carol**: Dale's sister. Never gets there.
- **Rhonda Pickett**: night server at the Starlite. Made the 911 call.
- **Bud Marchetti**: the Starlite's line cook. The walk-in hummed.
- **Walt Brzezinski**: the Starlite regular, third stool, eleven years.
- **Sgt. Hollis, Pvt. Anna Voss, Capt. R. Lindqvist**: the cordon.
- **Marguerite**: Survey Lead, Grid 4. **Dot**, **Hal**, **Scoutmaster Ortega**: her people.
- **Nadia, Hector, Priya**: the pharmacist, the produce manager, the owner of Mr. Biscuits.
- **Mr. Biscuits**: an orange cat. Do not let him lick you.

## How notes work

- 60 notes in 8 sets. A faction only ever finds (and counts) notes it can read: 52 each.
- Chances per source: supply cache 12%, landmark cache 25%, PvE win 3%, elite win 10%, airdrop 35%,
  raid kill 100% for every contributor (one note). First Blood always leaves *Dale's Last Text*.
- A note never drops twice. Landmark kinds lean towards sets: pharmacy to Project Lantern, diner
  to the Starlite, police to Containment; each Along the Road note comes only from its own kind of
  place (pharmacy, gas station, hardware store, grocery, outfitter).
- **Patient Zero** pieces drop only from airdrops, elites and raids, and only once the player holds
  at least half of every other set they can read. The set shows as "???" until the first piece.
- Completing a set pays salvage (the sum of its notes: common 10, uncommon 20, rare 40, legendary
  100) and a cosmetic title, shown in the profile, once per set.
