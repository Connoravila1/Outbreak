# OUTBREAK — MVP CATALOGUE AND PROGRESSION

This is the authored content boundary for the playable MVP. The systems are complete; the initial
catalogue is deliberately small enough to balance by hand.

## Progression contract

- Levels 1–10 are playable and persistent.
- XP comes from participation in real encounters, never distance travelled or map pickups.
- Every level adds one inventory slot, from 6 slots at level 1 to 15 at level 10.
- Higher levels widen the encounter-drop pool; they never create location-specific rewards.
- A new discovery enters inventory. A duplicate becomes salvage.
- Equipment can be changed only outside an encounter.
- Items affect the next authoritative combat resolution.
- Evidence is collectible lore. It occupies the catalogue but not equipment inventory capacity.

## Evidence collection

Evidence has no combat power and is never tied to a real-world location. It is encounter-issued
story material collected in the Record.

1. Torn Evacuation Order
2. Bent Quarantine Keycard
3. Bloodless Sample Tube
4. Burned Dispatch Tape
5. Redacted Patient Zero File
6. Original Containment Seal

## MVP reward presentation

An encounter reward is not another row in a report. The foreground sequence is:

> Result → XP count-up → level-up (when crossed) → item signal → rarity reveal → item reveal → keep/duplicate conversion → continue

The field test demonstrates this sequence without adding its synthetic rewards to the live record.
