Delete your Outbreak account

You can delete your Outbreak account and everything attached to it at any time. This is permanent.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Outbreak and go to the You tab.
  2. Scroll to Account and tap Delete account.
  3. Type DELETE to confirm.

The account is deleted on the server straight away and the app returns to its first screen.

If you cannot open the app

Reinstall Outbreak, sign in with the same Google or Apple account, and follow the steps above. Or email us from the address on your Google or Apple account and ask for deletion; include your callsign. We will confirm by reply. (Contact: privacy@example.com, placeholder: replace with the real address before publishing.)

Guest characters (no account linked) cannot be recovered after uninstalling, so there is nothing to request; in the app, Delete account removes the guest character from the server.

What is deleted

  • Your character: level, gear, consumables, missions, events, combat records.
  • Your sign-in identifiers and email address.
  • Your clan membership. If you led the clan, leadership passes to another member; if you were its only member, the clan is closed.
  • Your callsign becomes available again.

What stays

  • Clan chat messages you wrote, which remain visible to your former clan with the author shown as "[deleted]" (chat older than seven days is removed automatically anyway).
  • Combined faction war totals, which contain no player identifiers.

Location is never stored, so there is none to delete. Read the privacy policy for the full picture.
