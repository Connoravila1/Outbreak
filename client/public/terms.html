Outbreak terms of service

Version 1 · Last updated: October 2026

Outbreak is a free game you play by walking around in the real world. These terms are the agreement between you and the person who runs the server you play on (“we”). They are written to be read. If you do not agree with them, please do not play.

Who can play

You must be at least 13 years old. If you are under the age of majority where you live (18 in many places), you need a parent or guardian to be fine with you playing. We do not ask for your birth date to keep it; the app checks your age on your phone and does not send it to us.

Playing safely, and at your own risk

Outbreak makes you look at a screen while you move through real places. That is risky if you are not careful, so please:

  • Pay attention to traffic and your surroundings. Look up.
  • Never play while driving or cycling. The game will not start fights while you are moving fast, but that is a backstop, not permission.
  • Stay out of private property and anywhere you are not allowed. Nothing in the game is worth trespassing for.
  • Take care after dark, and go with other people when you can.
  • Stay out of sanctuaries (hospitals, schools and places of worship) and do not play in places where a game would be rude or dangerous.

You are responsible for where you go and what you do while playing. You play at your own risk. To the extent the law allows, we are not responsible for injury, loss or damage that happens while you play, or for anything you do in the real world because of the game.

Being decent

You write a few things other people can read: your callsign, clan names and tags, and clan chat. Keep them decent.

  • No slurs or obvious obscenities in names, including look-alike spellings. A name that is not allowed is refused, and a moderator can reset one that slipped through.
  • No harassment, threats, hate or abuse in clan chat or anywhere else in the game.
  • No cheating: no fake locations, no tools that play for you, no exploiting bugs, no helping others to do so.
  • Do not try to find out who the people you meet in a fight are, or where they live. Fights are anonymous on purpose.
  • Do not break or overload the server, or try to get into other people’s accounts.

You can report a callsign, a clan or a chat message from inside the game.

Moderation and suspension

We may reset a name, delete a message, or suspend or close an account, for a time or for good, if we think these terms were broken. You will be told the reason when you open the app. We try to be fair and to keep reasons short and civil, but the decision is ours. A suspended account keeps its progress until it is lifted or deleted.

Your account and your data

Your character lives on the game server. You can delete your account at any time (You, Settings, Account, Delete account), which removes your character and sign-in details. What we keep, and how your location is handled, is in the privacy policy. You can also sign in with Google or Apple; that is subject to their terms too.

The game itself

Outbreak is free. There is nothing to buy with real money. Salvage, gear, levels and everything else in the game have no value outside it and cannot be traded or sold. We may change, pause or end the game, a feature or a server at any time, and we may reset progress when the game needs it. We will try to give notice of big changes.

The game is provided as it is. We do not promise that it will always be available, accurate or free of bugs, and we are not liable for losing a character or for anything that follows from the game being down. If the law says we cannot exclude something, that part stays as the law says.
