Outbreak privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026

Outbreak is a location-based game. This page says plainly what the game stores about you, what it does not, and how to delete it.

What we store

  • Sign-in identifiers. If you sign in with Google or Apple, we keep the provider name and the account ID that provider gives us for this game. That ID is how we recognise you next time.
  • Your email address, only if the provider shares it. Apple may give us a private relay address instead of your real one. We show it to you in Settings. We do not use it to send anything.
  • Your callsign. The name you choose. Other players see it on leaderboards and in your clan, never attached to a real-world encounter.
  • Game progress. Your faction, level, gear, missions, clan membership, clan chat messages, combat records and war points.
  • Technical logs. The server records each request's method, path, result and timing. It does not log request bodies, sign-in tokens or positions. The web server in front of it may keep standard access logs, including IP addresses, for a short time.

Guests who never sign in have none of the first two items. Their character is tied only to a random token kept on their phone.

Location

The game needs your location to place enemies, caches and other players around you. Your position is sent to the server while you play and held in memory for about two minutes so the game can work. It is never written to the database, never shown to other players and never shared with anyone. Other players only ever meet you as an anonymous opponent.

What we do not do

  • No advertising and no ad or analytics SDKs.
  • We do not sell or rent your data, and we do not share it for marketing.
  • We do not track you across other apps or sites.
  • Signing in does not give us access to your Google or Apple account beyond the sign-in ID (and email, if shared).

Who processes the data

Google and Apple handle the sign-in itself, under their own privacy policies. Map tiles come from OpenFreeMap; your device requests them directly, so that service sees your device's IP address and the map area you view, like any map. Game data lives on the server that runs Outbreak.

Deleting your data

In the app: You, then Settings, then Account, then Delete account, and type DELETE. This permanently removes your character, gear, missions, events, sign-in IDs, email and clan membership from the server and clears the app's data on your phone. If you led a clan, leadership passes to another member; if you were alone in it, the clan is closed. Clan chat messages you wrote stay but are changed to "[deleted]". War points remain only as combined faction totals that identify no one.

If you cannot open the app, see how to delete your account without it.

Children

Outbreak is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you think a child has made an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, the date above changes and the app will say so.
